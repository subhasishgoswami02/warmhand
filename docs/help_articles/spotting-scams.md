# Spotting scams: what Kettlewick will never ask you

*Kettlewick Bank is fictional. This article is written for a demonstration.*

Last reviewed: 27th September 2026.

## What Kettlewick will never do

Kettlewick will never ask you for your full card number, your PIN, your security code, your password, or a one-time code we sent you. This is true whether you are contacted by phone, text, email, social media or in person, and it is true even if the person says they are from our fraud team.

Kettlewick will never ask you to move money to a different account to keep it safe. There is no such thing as a safe account at Kettlewick. Anyone who tells you otherwise is not from Kettlewick.

Kettlewick will never ask you to buy gift cards, send cryptocurrency, or withdraw cash and hand it to anyone, for any reason.

Kettlewick will never ask you to install software so that someone can see or control your screen.

Kettlewick will never pressure you to act immediately or tell you to keep a conversation secret from your family or from our staff.

## How to check whether a message is really from us

Do not use a phone number, link or email address that came in the message itself. Scammers supply their own contact details.

Instead, close the message and reach us through the app, or through the phone number printed on the back of your card. If the first contact was genuine, nothing is lost by hanging up and calling back that way.

Caller ID can be faked. A call that appears to come from Kettlewick's number may not be from Kettlewick.

## If you are on the phone with someone right now

Stop. Do not send money, and do not share codes, passwords or card details. End the call and reach Kettlewick through the app or the number on your card.

## If you think you have already been targeted

Tell us. Use the app or the number on the back of your card, and say that you think you have been scammed. A person will take it from there.

You can freeze your card yourself in the app under Cards, then Freeze card, while you wait.

## What this article does not cover

This article describes how to recognise a scam. It does not tell you what will happen with a particular payment, whether money can be recovered, or who is responsible for a loss. Those depend on the specific circumstances and a Kettlewick agent needs to look at them with you.
