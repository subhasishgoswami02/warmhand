# Your statement, your due date, and how interest works

*Kettlewick Bank is fictional. Every figure below is invented for a demonstration.*

Last reviewed: 27th September 2026.

## When your statement closes

Your credit card statement closes on the same date each month. That date is set when the account is opened and does not change. You can see it in the app under Card, then Statements.

Your payment is due 25 days after the statement closes. The due date is printed at the top of every statement and shown in the app.

If the due date falls on a day Kettlewick does not process payments, a payment made on the next processing day is treated as on time.

## The grace period on purchases

Kettlewick does not charge interest on new purchases if the statement balance is paid in full by the due date. This is called a grace period.

If the statement balance is not paid in full, interest is charged on purchases from the day each purchase posted, and the grace period does not apply again until the balance is paid in full and stays paid for a full statement period.

## Where there is no grace period

Cash advances and balance transfers begin accruing interest on the day they post. Paying the statement balance in full does not avoid interest on these.

## How interest is calculated

Kettlewick uses the average daily balance method. The balance is recorded at the end of each day in the statement period, those balances are averaged, and the daily rate is applied. The daily rate is the annual percentage rate divided by 365.

Your annual percentage rate is shown on your statement and in the app under Card, then Rates and fees. Different rates can apply to purchases, cash advances and balance transfers on the same card.

## Minimum payment

The minimum payment is the greater of $35 or 2% of the statement balance, plus any interest and fees charged in that period. Paying only the minimum means the remaining balance continues to accrue interest.

## Checking account statements

Checking and savings statements cover a calendar month and are available in the app within three business days of the month ending. Statements are kept in the app for seven years.

## What this article does not cover

This article explains how statements and interest work at Kettlewick in general. It does not explain the interest charged on a particular account in a particular month, and it cannot confirm whether a specific charge or interest amount is correct. A Kettlewick agent needs the account open in front of them to answer that.
