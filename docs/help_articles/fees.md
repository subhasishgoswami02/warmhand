# Fees at Kettlewick

*Kettlewick Bank is fictional. Every figure below is invented for a demonstration.*

Last reviewed: 27th September 2026.

## Checking and savings

| Fee | Amount | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly maintenance | $6 | Charged on the last business day of each statement period |
| Overdraft | $29 | Per item paid into overdraft, maximum two per day |
| Returned item | $29 | Per item returned unpaid, maximum two per day |
| Out of network ATM | $3 | Plus whatever the ATM operator charges |
| Outgoing domestic wire | $25 | Per wire |
| Paper statement | $2 | Per statement, per month |

The monthly maintenance fee is waived for the whole statement period if either the average daily balance is $1,500 or more, or a direct deposit of $500 or more posts during that period. Only one condition needs to be met.

No overdraft fee is charged when the account is overdrawn by $5 or less at the end of the day.

Kettlewick ATMs and Allpoint network ATMs are free to use.

## Credit card

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Annual fee | $0 |
| Foreign transaction | 3% of the transaction amount in US dollars |
| Late payment | Up to $32, or up to $41 if a payment was also late in the previous six billing cycles |
| Cash advance | 5% of the amount advanced, minimum $10 |
| Balance transfer | 4% of the amount transferred, minimum $10 |
| Returned payment | Up to $32 |

## Where to see the fees you were charged

Fees appear on your statement under Fees charged, and in the app under Activity with a Fee label. To find a specific fee, open the statement for the month it was charged.

## What this article does not cover

This article lists what Kettlewick charges. It does not explain a fee on a particular account, and it cannot tell you whether a fee you were charged was correct. If a fee looks wrong to you, a Kettlewick agent needs to look at the account.
