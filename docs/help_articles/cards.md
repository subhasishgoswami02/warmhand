# Freezing, replacing and using your card

*Kettlewick Bank is fictional. Every figure below is invented for a demonstration.*

Last reviewed: 27th September 2026.

## Freezing your card

You can freeze a card yourself in the app at any time. Open Cards, choose the card, and tap Freeze card.

Freezing takes effect immediately and stops new purchases, ATM withdrawals and cash advances on that card. It does not stop recurring payments you have already set up with a merchant, and it does not stop transactions that were authorised before you froze it.

You can unfreeze the card in the same place. Freezing and unfreezing is free and you can do it as often as you like.

Freezing a card is not the same as reporting it lost or stolen, and it is not the same as reporting a transaction you did not make. If either of those applies, a Kettlewick agent needs to take it from here.

## Replacing your card

Order a replacement in the app under Cards, then Replace card.

| Delivery | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5 to 7 business days | Free |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $25 |

A replacement card has a new card number, a new expiry date and a new security code. Any recurring payments set up with the old number need to be updated with the merchant.

## Daily limits

| Limit | Amount |
|---|---|
| ATM withdrawal | $600 per day |
| Debit card purchases | $2,500 per day |
| Mobile check deposit | $5,000 per day |

Limits reset at midnight Eastern Time. A Kettlewick agent can discuss a temporary change to a limit.

## Using your card abroad

Tell Kettlewick your travel dates in the app under Cards, then Travel notice, so that foreign transactions are not held for review. A foreign transaction fee of 3% applies to purchases made in a currency other than US dollars.

## What this article does not cover

This article explains what the card controls do. It does not cover a transaction on a particular account, whether a specific charge was authorised, or what happens after a card is reported lost or stolen. Those need a Kettlewick agent.
