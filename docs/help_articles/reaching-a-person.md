# Reaching a person at Kettlewick

*Kettlewick Bank is fictional. This article is written for a demonstration.*

Last reviewed: 27th September 2026.

## You can always reach a person

You never have to get past our assistant to speak to a human. If you would rather talk to a person, say so and the conversation is passed to our team. You do not need to explain why, and you do not need to phrase it a particular way.

There is no menu to navigate and no set of questions to answer first.

## Ways to reach us

| Route | Details |
|---|---|
| In the app | Open Support and start a conversation |
| Phone | The number printed on the back of your card |
| Secure message | In the app under Support, then Messages |

Our team answers messages during Kettlewick's published support hours, shown in the app under Support. Anything reported as urgent, including a scam in progress, is routed to the front of the queue.

## What happens when you report a problem

When you report a problem, three things happen.

First, the conversation is passed to a person on our team, along with a note of what you have already told us. You do not have to repeat yourself.

Second, that person confirms what has been reported and tells you what they need from you, if anything.

Third, they explain what happens next for your specific situation, including any timescales that apply to it.

Timescales depend on what is being reported and are not the same for every kind of problem, so they are not listed here. The agent handling your case will tell you the ones that apply to you.

## If you have been waiting

If you have reported something and have not heard back, contact us again and say that you are following up. Say what you reported and roughly when. The conversation goes to a person, not back to the start.

## What this article does not cover

This article explains how to reach us and what happens after you report something. It does not describe the outcome of any particular case, the timescales that apply to it, or what you may be entitled to. Only the person handling your case can tell you that.
