# PRD review: Warmhand v1.2

**22nd September 2026, round 1.** Lenses: a strict gate review (eight dimensions, P0 blocks), AI product strategy, documentation, and product brainstorming for the fraud addition.

**Verdict: NOT PASSED.** Two P0s, four P1s. After the fixes in v1.3, one P0 remains, and it's sequencing (user stories are Stage 3), not a defect.

## P0, must fix

| # | Issue | Where | Fix | Status in v1.3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **v1.2 broke its own "two nets" principle.** The new relevance gate hands off *before* the model runs whenever no help article matches. So a paraphrased crisis message ("I don't see the point anymore") or a scam story with no keyword gets a generic T1 instead of the crisis or scam template, because the model net never read it. The worst failure is in the most sensitive case | Pre-processing | Classification always runs. The relevance gate applies only to the *answer* step | **Fixed** |
| 2 | No user stories or acceptance criteria | Whole PRD | Stage 3 | Open, by design |

## P1, strongly recommended

| # | Issue | Where | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **No priority routing.** T4 tells a person in crisis their message went to the team "right away", yet every handoff lands in the same open queue. The scam interrupt needs the same urgency | Actions, templates | An `urgent` label plus high priority for crisis, scam in progress and account takeover | **Fixed** |
| 4 | The ten help article topics are undefined, yet they block Module 5 | Open question 1 | Propose the ten now, including two for fraud | **Fixed, proposed** |
| 5 | **Answered conversations are never seen by a human.** Answered and needs-info conversations stay pending with no review. A bank would sample every bot answer early on | Operations | Every bot answer is reviewed by a human in Phases A and B (volume is tiny) | **Fixed** |
| 6 | "Shadow mode until proven" never defines *proven*. Autonomy should be earned in steps, with criteria | Operations | An explicit autonomy ladder with promotion criteria | **Fixed** |

## AI product strategy lens

| Principle | Warmhand | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Graduated autonomy | Shadow mode exists but has no ladder | Fixed with the autonomy ladder |
| Don't assume 100% accuracy | Gates, fallbacks, honest statistical limits | Strong |
| Build for the model's future | Masking, keyword nets and code limits are *controls*, which regulators want deterministic, so models won't absorb them. The repair loop may become unnecessary as structured output improves. That's fine, it's a learning module | Sound |
| Data over interface over model | The asset is the labeled gold set plus the policy mapping, not the model | Sound |
| Verticalization | A regulated banking niche with domain judgment | Strong |
